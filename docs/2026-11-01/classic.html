Ghostty benefits developers, power users, and organizations seeking a responsive terminal with advanced workflows such as tabs, panes, splits, multi-window operation, and platform-specific integrations. Kronos is an open-source foundation-model family designed to understand and forecast financial candlestick, or K-line, data across markets and exchanges. It converts continuous OHLCV market sequences into hierarchical discrete tokens using a specialized tokenizer, then models those tokens with decoder-only autoregressive Transformers. This design targets the noisy, multidimensional nature of financial time series and supports forecasting across different assets and time intervals. The repository provides pretrained models ranging from a 4.1-million-parameter mini version to a 102.3-million-parameter base version, along with Hugging Face model access, prediction utilities, batch inference, visualization examples, and fine-tuning scripts. Its `KronosPredictor` handles preprocessing, normalization, forecasting, inverse transformation, and probabilistic sampling, while an accompanying Qlib-based example demonstrates fine-tuning and backtesting on Chinese A-share data.

The project is particularly useful for quantitative researchers, financial engineers, traders, and developers building market-analysis or forecasting systems without training a foundation model from scratch. Its pretrained models can accelerate experimentation with asset forecasting, portfolio research, market simulation, and task-specific adaptation, although the repository cautions that its examples are not production-ready trading systems. Users benefit from accessible model sizes, support for optional volume and amount fields, GPU-enabled batch prediction, and an open-source fine-tuning workflow. Kronos is attracting attention because it applies modern foundation-model techniques to a specialized financial representation rather than generic time-series data.

Keyboard font

Here is a pleasingly specific act of digital preservation:

"A font recreated from photographs of classic Commodore 64 keycaps, with cleaned-up shapes and consistent proportions."

This is not the Commodore 64 screen font. It is the font printed on the physical keys that you used to type characters that the Commodore 64 would then display in a different font. The computer has been gone for decades, so naturally we are reconstructing its keyboard labels from photographs for use on modern computers. Technological progress is mostly about developing increasingly powerful machines on which to imitate the typography of less powerful machines.

The font includes uppercase letters, numbers, punctuation, classic key legends and all 63 PETSCII graphic front legends. Lowercase input displays as uppercase, because that is what the keys looked like. If you want lowercase, buy a newer computer, I guess.

AI mathematics

OpenAI says it is releasing "a broad range of new mathematical results produced by an internal frontier model." The results will go on GitHub, many of the proofs are being formalized in Lean so computers can check them, and OpenAI is publishing "10 summaries of the model's reasoning," compute estimates and statistics about attempted problems. The average result used roughly three hours of ChatGPT Pro thinking.

There is an old-fashioned model of mathematics where a mathematician thinks for months, writes a paper, explains the proof to other mathematicians and submits it to a journal. The new model is that an internal AI thinks for three hours, produces a result, another computer checks the proof, and OpenAI funds workshops so humans can understand what happened.

OpenAI says it is "continuing to explore other community-hosted alternatives" for releasing the work and is committed to improving "the citations, mathematical exposition, and presentation of the results." Sure. The machine has solved the theorem; now comes the harder problem of getting academics to agree on where to post it and how to cite it.

Technical Analysis

The project is an unofficial font that reconstructs the lettering and symbols printed on classic Commodore 64 keycaps from photographs, refining them into consistent, clean outlines rather than reproducing the C64’s screen bitmap font. It includes standard characters, accented letters, function-key labels, classic legends, and all 63 PETSCII graphic symbols, with downloadable TTF, OTF, and webfont formats. Its importance is primarily cultural and practical: it preserves a distinctive piece of retro-computing design in a usable, distributable digital typeface.

How One Molecule Gets the Winning Hand

nobelprize.org  ·  37 pts  ·  4 comments

Kagan and Kenso Soai receive the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for explaining how molecular handedness can amplify itself. Their work connects asymmetric synthesis to the chemistry of life and the design of medicines.

Read →Share →
[ Analysis + ]

Technical Analysis

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences has awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry to Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai for discovering nonlinear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis. Their work explains how homochirality—the predominance of one molecular “handedness,” as seen in biological amino acids—can arise spontaneously rather than producing equal mirror-image mixtures. Kagan’s 1986 findings enabled much larger chiral imbalances, while Soai developed a reaction that ultimately produced only one mirror image. The discoveries are important both for understanding the chemical origins of life and for designing pharmaceuticals, where the two molecular forms can have very different biological effects.

Technical Analysis

Its importance lies in enabling inexpensive, fast decision-making alongside larger language models, potentially reducing latency and cost in agentic workflows, though it is unsuitable for open-ended reasoning or text generation.

Technical Analysis

OpenAI has launched the Decisions API in public beta, a specialized endpoint designed to evaluate text and images and return typed answers roughly 10 times faster than the Responses API. Using the currently available gpt-6-luna model, developers can ask predicate questions that return probabilities, choice questions that select from fixed categories, or score questions that assess ordered rubrics with probability distributions and confidence values. Embeddings Slip the Cloud’s Leash

blog.google  ·  338 pts  ·  35 comments

EmbeddingGemma 2 places text, code, images, video, and audio in a shared vector space. Its open license, quantized inference, and consumer-hardware footprint make local, privacy-preserving retrieval a practical target rather than a distant promise.

Technical Analysis

Google has released EmbeddingGemma 2, an Apache 2.0-licensed, 740-million-parameter multimodal embedding model that maps text, code, images, video, and audio into a shared vector space. Designed for consumer hardware, it supports modular text, vision, and audio components, an 8K-token context window, quantized low-memory inference, and variable-length embeddings that can reduce storage by up to six times. The model improves substantially over its predecessor on code retrieval while targeting strong performance across multilingual text and other modalities, enabling local semantic search, cross-modal retrieval, and privacy-preserving RAG. Its availability through common deployment and machine-learning tools gives developers an open alternative for building offline, on-device search and retrieval systems without sending data to cloud services.

Analysis

Google has released EmbeddingGemma 2, an Apache 2.0-licensed, 740-million-parameter multimodal embedding model that maps text, code, images, video, and audio into a shared vector space. Designed for consumer hardware, it supports modular text, vision, and audio components, an 8K-token context window, quantized low-memory inference, and variable-length embeddings that can reduce storage by up to six times. ESP32-C3 Adblock

github.com  ·  107 pts  ·  41 comments  ·  discussion

Analysis

ESP32-C3 Adblock is an open-source, Pi-hole-style DNS sinkhole that runs on an inexpensive $2 ESP32-C3 without PSRAM, blocking ads and trackers for an entire network. Its key optimization is storing sorted 40-bit hashes of more than 140,000 domains in flash and binary-searching them, using roughly 50 KB of RAM and answering lookups in about 10 milliseconds. The project includes Wi-Fi setup, a web dashboard, customizable blocklists, and OTA firmware and data updates, though 4 MB flash limits the balance between large lists and dual-slot firmware updates. It is notable because it makes network-wide ad blocking practical on much cheaper, smaller hardware, while also documenting collision risks and important LAN-security limitations such as unauthenticated HTTP transport and an initially open setup portal.

What is Codemode

lucumr.pocoo.org  ·  92 pts  ·  43 comments  ·  discussion

Analysis

The article explains Codemode, a Pi feature that lets an AI agent write and execute sandboxed JavaScript on the trusted harness side to compose tool calls, rather than exposing every tool directly in the model’s context. This separates orchestration from the sandboxed execution environment and enables concurrency, stateful workflows, structured processing of large outputs, and access to capabilities such as image generation, classifiers, subagents, and MCP services. The approach can reduce context consumption and make complex automation more flexible than standalone CLI calls, while retaining CLIs for operations in the target environment. Its effectiveness with MCP currently depends on structured, consistent results, better binary-data support, and improved tool discovery, and open issues include durability, security boundaries, and support for smaller models.
